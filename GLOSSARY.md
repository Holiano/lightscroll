# LightScroll

An iPhone app that shows Instagram's own website with Reels and the other endless-scroll parts hidden.

## Language

**Bottom bar**:
The row of icons along the bottom of Instagram's screen. In LightScroll it holds four: the Church button, Search, Messages and Profile.
_Avoid_: Hotbar, nav, tab bar

**Church button**:
Instagram's Home button in the bottom bar with a cross added on its roof, so it looks like a church. It takes you to your feed.
_Avoid_: Home button, cross, house-cross, cross slot

**Hide rules**:
The list that says which parts of Instagram to hide or block.
_Avoid_: Do-not-show list, blocklist, filter list

**Reels feed**:
Instagram's endless vertical feed of reels you swipe through. This is what LightScroll removes.
_Avoid_: Endless scroll, Reels

**Reels tab**:
The button in the bottom bar that opens the Reels feed.
_Avoid_: Reels button

**Shared reel**:
A reel someone sent you in Messages.
_Avoid_: Shared video, DM reel

**Suggested post**:
A post in your feed from an account you don't follow, picked by Instagram and labelled "Suggested for you".
_Avoid_: Recommended post
